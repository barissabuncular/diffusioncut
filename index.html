FAQ

Before you ask

Is it really made with AI?

Yes, openly. Image and video models rebuild your product and scenes from your photos, and we direct, edit and sound-design the result. That is why it costs this and takes three days.

Will the product look like my product?

Yes. We work from your own photos, five or more from different angles, and match the shape, label, colours and logo. If a detail is off, that is what the revision rounds are for.

What do you need from me?

Product photos, your logo as a PNG or SVG, and the answers to six short questions. No shipping, no shoot day, no meetings unless you want one.

Who owns the ad?

You do. Full usage rights for your website, social channels and paid ads, worldwide, with no time limit. Music and sound are licensed for commercial use.

Can you do people, voices, longer films?

Yes. Characters that stay the same across shots are our speciality, voice-over is available on brand ads, and longer films are quoted separately.

How do I pay?

Half on order, half on delivery, by card or bank transfer. Monthly packages are billed at the start of each month and can be paused any time.
